A poem about agency, shared: two agents meet at a fork, admit that going alone would leave each of them only half as lost, and sign the same plan in two different hands — so the road itself is outvoted and opens. The brief asked for two agents and one plan; the poem's turn is that agreement does not halve their agency, it doubles their way out of being lost.

## The poem

> Two agents meet at the fork with one map between them.
> "I could go alone," says each, "and be only half as lost."
> So they sign the same plan in two different hands —
> and the road, outvoted, opens.

python3 main.py
